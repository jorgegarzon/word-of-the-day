# 2026-11-23: v3 vs v2 A/B test (Nov 24 – Dec 31)

Decided by Jorge on 2026-11-23.

## Why

Instagram insights showed about **50% of viewers dropping at the 1-second mark** on the v2 reels.
Metricool agrees: the 3-second skip rate (IGRE30) was 79.6% (nincompoop) and 84.1% (rigmarole),
vs 77.9% on average for v1. In v2 the first second is a still frame: hook, word, empty middle and the
"Follow" line; option A only pops in at 0.6s, B and C at 1.2s and 1.8s, and the audio fades in.

## What v3 changes (`render.py --short` with `"v3": true` in the word JSON)

| Time | On screen |
|---|---|
| 0.0s | "What does this word mean? 🤔", the word and **all 3 options**, already visible; the word grows in and the options settle over 0.3s; a pop sound at 0.0s |
| 0.6 / 1.4 / 2.2s | Countdown 3, 2, 1 with a clock tick |
| 3.0s | Answer turns green; the wrong options dim |
| 3.3s | Example sentence and "Follow for your word of the day 🔔" (the CTA is no longer on screen from the start) |
| 5.4s | Loops back to the start |

Unchanged: words, options, answers, examples, captions (the hook stays in the caption, it's just no
longer on screen), first comments, hashtags and publish times.

## The split

From Nov 24, posts alternate v3 / v2 by day, starting with v3: 19 v3 and 19 v2 posts. The split is
balanced on weekends (5 each) and close on hook style (v3: 12 surprise, 3 challenge, 2 relatable,
2 tag; v2: 13 surprise, 2 challenge, 2 relatable, 2 tag).

- Batch: `batch/2026-nov-dec-v3.py` (input: the v2 batch JSON).
- Videos: `videos/wotd-<date>-<word>-v3.mp4`. The v2 days keep their `-v2.mp4` video untouched.
- `words_used.json`: `"format"` is `"v3-5.4s"` or `"v2-6.6s"`, and every post in the test has
  `"ab_test": "v3-vs-v2 (from 2026-11-24)"`. `metrics.json` copies `format` from there.
- Metricool: the 19 v3 posts were updated in place (same uuids) with the new video, on Instagram and Facebook.

## How to judge it

Main metric: **skip rate (IGRE30)** and view rate (IGRE28), then avg watch time as a share of the
video length, views and follower gains. The 1-second drop is only in Instagram's own insights, not
Metricool: if Jorge sends it per reel, store it as `instagram.drop_1s` in `metrics.json`.
Wait for at least 4 non-provisional posts per format before calling it; a fair read needs ~2 weeks.
