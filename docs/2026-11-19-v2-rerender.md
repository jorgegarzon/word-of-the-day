# 2026-11-19: why we re-rendered Nov 20 – Dec 31 (v2)

Written after a chat between Jorge and Claude on 2026-11-19. It records what had performed well
so far and why the 42 scheduled posts were re-rendered and rescheduled.

## What we looked at (Instagram, 12 reels, Nov 7–18)

Followers: 1 (Nov 7) → 5 (Nov 14) → 5 (Nov 18). No gains in the last 4 days.

Other findings:

- **Weekend posts did worst.** persnickety (Sat) got 39 views and gallivant (Sun) got 23. Weekday posts averaged 123.
- **People leave early.** The skip rate (viewers who swipe away within 3 seconds) averaged 80.7%, ranging from 71% to 91%. Avg watch time fell from 5.9s on Nov 7–8 to about 3.1s on Nov 9–18. The old video was 15s, with the reveal at 7s.
- **The score in metrics.json is misleading.** Every reel has exactly 1 comment, which is almost certainly our own first comment. So the score is mostly 1,000 ÷ views, and the reels with the fewest views rank highest. gallivant (23 views) has the top score but is the weakest reel. MEASURE.md hasn't been changed yet.
- **Facebook** sometimes beats Instagram on views (flabbergasted 280, skedaddle 201), but Metricool doesn't report Facebook comments or shares.
- **Caveat:** with only 12 posts and almost no engagement, none of these differences is statistically reliable. They are leads to test.

## What we changed (v2)

1. **More surprise hooks, each with an emoji**, in the petrichor pattern: "This sounds like X. It's not 🦕". The 42 posts are 28 surprise, 5 relatable, 5 challenge and 4 tag. The hook is shown on screen as well as used in the caption.
2. **A short looping video instead of 15s.** Jorge tuned the timing by watching samples (2s, then 2.5s, 3.3s, 3.6s, 4.6s, 5.6s, and finally 6.6s):

   | Time | On screen |
   |---|---|
   | 0.0s | Hook and word |
   | 0.6 / 1.2 / 1.8s | Options A, B and C pop in |
   | 2.4 / 3.0 / 3.6s | Countdown 3, 2, 1, each with a clock tick |
   | 4.2s | Answer turns green; the wrong options dim |
   | 4.6s | Example sentence, on screen for 2s |
   | 6.6s | Loops back to the start |

   "Follow for your word of the day 🔔" is on screen the whole time, at the bottom in white italics.
3. **Weekend posts move to 12:00 MST** (13:00 Mexico City). Weekdays stay at 10:00 Mexico City.
4. **Unchanged:** words, options, answers, example sentences, first comments and topic hashtags.

whippersnapper (Nov 19) had already gone out in the old 15s format. v2 starts with nincompoop on Nov 20.
